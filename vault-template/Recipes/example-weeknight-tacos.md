# Weeknight Tacos

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 8 corn tortillas
- 1 packet taco seasoning, or make your own
- toppings: shredded cheese, lettuce, salsa, lime

## Steps

1. Brown the beef, drain, stir in the seasoning with a splash of water.
2. Warm the tortillas.
3. Build at the table.
